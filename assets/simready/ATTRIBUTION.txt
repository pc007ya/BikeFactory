Changes made 2026-10-27 for the bicycle production-line prototype:
- Converted original composed OpenUSD visual geometry/materials to GLB.
- Retained geometry shape, normals, UVs, material subsets and PreviewSurface texture bindings.
- Triangulated and generated tangents; no mesh decimation or substitute modeling.
- Grounded/centered the origin; changed Z-up to Y-up; kept meter scale.
- Resized PBR textures to max 512px and embedded them in each GLB.
- Converted shader representation to glTF PBR.
- Omitted USD physics/semantic schemas and runtime variants. GLBs are VISUAL ONLY, not SimReady-certified simulation assets.
