- Converted original composed OpenUSD visuals to self-contained GLB.
- Retained all geometry; no decimation, fake replacement meshes, or altered proportions.
- Retained source UVs, normals, material subsets, PBR constants and texture transforms.
- Triangulated and generated tangents; merged static meshes and identical materials losslessly.
